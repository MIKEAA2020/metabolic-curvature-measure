\begin{proposition}[Fourth perturbation axis: phosphate-limited medium]
\label{prop:keio-phosphate}
Limiting the phosphate exchange of the glucose-only medium ---
the phosphate axis, protocol identical, labels declared at $5\%$ of
each level's own wild type --- with
$\mathrm{EX\_pi\_e}\in\{-0.5,-0.25,-0.1\}$ on iJO1366 and
$\{-0.25,-0.1\}$ on iML1515 drops the wild-type optima from $0.982$
to $0.518$, $0.259$, $0.104$ and from $0.822$ to $0.259$, $0.104$
($-47/-74/-89\%$ and $-68/-87\%$). The essentiality sets are
unchanged at every level above the floor: $289/1{,}367$ and
$286/1{,}516$ at $-0.5$ and $-0.25$ in both reconstructions, zero
flips, $\kappa=1.000$. At the $-89\%$/$-87\%$ floors the only
motion is the same marginal family every $-90\%$ floor below finds:
\emph{fabZ} and \emph{bioH} lost on iJO1366 ($287$,
$\kappa=0.996$), those two plus \emph{bioD} on iML1515 ($283$,
$\kappa=0.994$); gains exactly zero. These floor losses are
numerical, not biological: the biomass coefficient of biotin is
$2\times10^{-6}$, so at a wild-type optimum of $0.104$ the demanded
flux through the governed reactions (\texttt{PMEACPE} for
\emph{bioH}, \texttt{DBTS} for \emph{bioD}) collapses to
$2.07\times10^{-7}$~mmol/gDW/h --- the boundary of the LP
feasibility tolerance --- and the returned knockout solutions
violate the zeroed bounds within tolerance, carrying exactly that
sub-tolerance flux and full growth (retention $1.000$); the
\emph{fabZ} case is in addition solve-order-dependent (a fresh
solve ordering returns it lethal, $b_{\mathrm{KO}}=0$). The
plain-FBA association collapses as the second supply side
predicts: transitive $r=+0.323$, $+0.054$, $-0.100$ (iJO1366) and
$+0.025$, $+0.096$ (iML1515) as limitation deepens, with
at-optimum phosphoglucose-isomerase variability $123$--$202$ (the
carbon-sector degeneracy signature of
Proposition~\ref{prop:keio-n-source}). Canonical selection
restores it at every level: $r=+0.950$, $+0.916$, $+0.914$
(iJO1366; held-out AUC $0.988$, $0.988$, $0.985$; MCC $0.965$,
$0.965$, $0.945$) and $+0.910$, $+0.900$ (iML1515; AUC $0.986$),
the canonical rankings correlated $+0.765$--$+0.968$ with the
baseline canonical ranking, the direct arm stable ($r\approx+0.35$
iJO1366, $+0.42$ iML1515), and the plain-versus-canonical
arm-agreement $\kappa=1.000$ at four of five levels ($0.993$,
$0.996$ at the floors).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Fifth perturbation axis: iron-limited medium]
\label{prop:keio-iron}
Limiting the ferrous-iron exchange --- the trace-metal axis,
selected by pre-screen over the seven cationic trace metals: iron
demand at the glucose-only optimum is $0.0158$ (iJO1366) and
$0.0132$ (iML1515)~mmol/gDW/h, $50$--$2{,}500\times$ every other
trace-metal demand, and the cation axes are interchangeable
proportional gradients --- with
$\mathrm{EX\_fe2\_e}\in\{-0.008,-0.004,-0.0016\}$ on iJO1366 and
$\{-0.0066,-0.0033,-0.0013\}$ on iML1515 drops the wild-type
optima from $0.982$ to $0.498$, $0.249$, $0.100$ and from $0.822$
to $0.411$, $0.205$, $0.081$ ($-49/-75/-90\%$ in both). The
essentiality sets are unchanged through $-75\%$ in both
reconstructions ($289/1{,}367$ and $286/1{,}516$, zero flips,
$\kappa=1.000$); at the $-90\%$ floors the losses are the
tolerance-floor family of
Proposition~\ref{prop:keio-phosphate} --- \emph{fabZ} and
\emph{bioH} on iJO1366 ($287$, $\kappa=0.996$), \emph{bioD} and
\emph{fabZ} on iML1515 ($284$, $\kappa=0.996$) --- with gains
exactly zero. The plain-FBA association again collapses along the
gradient: $r=+0.404$, $+0.220$, $-0.108$ (iJO1366) and $+0.271$,
$+0.231$, $+0.040$ (iML1515), phosphoglucose-isomerase variability
$126$--$202$. Canonical selection restores it at every level:
$r=+0.947$, $+0.913$, $+0.910$ (iJO1366; AUC $0.988$, $0.988$,
$0.982$; MCC $0.965$, $0.965$, $0.926$) and $+0.925$, $+0.907$,
$+0.892$ (iML1515; AUC $0.986$, $0.986$, $0.981$), canonical
rankings correlated $+0.616$--$+0.968$ with the baseline canonical
ranking, arm agreement $\kappa=1.000$ at five of six levels
($0.991$, $0.996$ at the floors).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Sixth perturbation axis: non-medium
maintenance stress]
\label{prop:keio-atpm}
Genome-scale flux balance has no temperature or pH state variable;
the standard constraint-based surrogate for temperature-style
stress is escalation of the non-growth-associated ATP maintenance
demand (\texttt{ATPM} lower bound; defaults $3.15$ iJO1366 /
$6.86$ iML1515~mmol ATP/gDW/h). A pre-screen compared this
encoding head-to-head with proton-leak forcing (the pH-style
surrogate): the maintenance axis spans $29$--$90\%$ wild-type
reduction with four-to-five usable levels in both models, while
the proton-leak axis compresses to two usable levels in iJO1366
(the proton leaves partly for free with organic-acid secretion)
before hard infeasibility --- ATPM is selected, and the axis
carries a decisive structural property: \emph{zero} wild-type
carbon-sector degeneracy, phosphoglucose-isomerase width $0.0$ at
every level in both models against $45$--$202$ on every supply
axis. Levels $\{40,60,80,100\}$ (iJO1366) and $\{60,80,100\}$
(iML1515) drop the wild-type optima from $0.982$ to $0.695$,
$0.496$, $0.298$, $0.099$ and from $0.822$ to $0.398$, $0.239$,
$0.080$. The labels re-stratify in one direction on this axis at
every level: the nine ATP-synthase genes of the \emph{atp} operon
(\emph{atpA}--\emph{atpI}) are gained already at the mildest level
($289\to298$, $286\to295$; $\kappa=0.981$, $0.981$), and at the
$-90\%$ endpoints the gain set explodes to $42$/$43$ genes ---
the \emph{atp} operon, the cytochrome-$bo_3$ operon
(\emph{cyoA}--\emph{cyoD}), the thirteen-gene NDH-I operon
(\emph{nuoA}--\emph{nuoN}), lower glycolysis (\emph{pgk},
\emph{eno}), \emph{ackA}, \emph{lpd}, \emph{tdcD}, \emph{purT},
and outer-membrane porins (\emph{ompG}, \emph{ompL}); oxidative
phosphorylation is enriched twenty-six-fold over the genome share
($26$ vs.\ $3.2$ expected), the mirror image of the anaerobic
glycolysis enrichment of
Proposition~\ref{prop:keio-o2-limited}. The losses at the
endpoints are the tolerance-floor family only (\emph{fabZ},
\emph{bioH}, plus \emph{bioD} on iML1515). The association splits
by arm exactly as the degeneracy reading predicts, with one
refinement. Plain FBA is \emph{strong at moderate stress} ---
$r=+0.949$, $+0.925$, $+0.821$ (iJO1366), all above the baseline
$+0.603$ --- and collapses only at the $-90\%$ endpoints:
$r=+0.453$ (iJO1366), $+0.234$ with held-out AUC $0.563$
(iML1515). Canonical selection restores the endpoint fully on
iJO1366 ($r=+0.937$, AUC $0.991$, MCC $0.974$; moderate levels
$+0.969$, $+0.949$, $+0.964$) and restores the ranking separation
but not the transitive correlation on iML1515 ($r=+0.703$,
$+0.655$, $+0.476$; AUC $0.992$, $0.992$, $0.978$; labels
$\kappa=1.000$, $1.000$, $0.992$) --- the near-tie boundary of
Remark~\ref{rem:canonical-scope}. Since the wild-type optimum is
unique at every level on this axis, the plain endpoint collapse
establishes that wild-level flux-variability width is a sufficient
but not necessary indicator: the operative mechanism is per-KO
vertex instability at near-zero-biomass optima, which canonical
selection repairs wherever the parsimony stage is discriminating.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[What the six axes establish together]
\label{rem:keio-multiaxis}
Five medium-side supply axes --- carbon source, electron acceptor,
nitrogen, phosphate, iron --- and one non-medium maintenance-stress
axis now bracket the claim from both sides. On the supply side the
labels are unconditionally invariant along the limitation gradients:
all fifteen gradient levels (ammonium, oxygen, phosphate, iron) in
both reconstructions show zero flips, $\kappa=1.000$, through
$-76\%$ wild-type reduction; the apparent floor losses at
$-89$--$-90\%$ are demonstrated tolerance-boundary effects rather
than re-stratification
(Proposition~\ref{prop:keio-phosphate}). The two genuine
re-stratifications are both regime switches confined to the module
the perturbation rewires: the anaerobic switch gains the
substrate-level-phosphorylation module
(Proposition~\ref{prop:keio-o2-limited}); the maintenance-demand
switch gains the energy-transduction module, and does so already
at mild stress --- a $40$~mmol/gDW/h ATP demand makes the ATP
synthase itself load-bearing. The nitrogen-substitution losses of
Proposition~\ref{prop:keio-n-source} remain the only
re-wiring-driven \emph{losses}, and they are substitution-mediated
in every case. The association, finally, is the invariant object
under canonical selection on all five supply axes in both models
(twenty-four levels, canonical $r\ge+0.856$, held-out AUC
$\ge0.978$), is restored on the maintenance axis in iJO1366, and
acquires its measured boundary on the maintenance axis in iML1515
--- to which the protocol subsection now turns.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Canonical flux selection as the invariant-object
protocol}\label{sec:canonical-selection}

The six-axis battery motivates promoting the canonical-selection
finding from a remark inside the nitrogen axis
(Remark~\ref{rem:keio-n-invariance}) to a protocol statement of
its own, because it is now measured across thirty-one levels, six
axes, and two reconstructions, and because its boundary is as
informative as its interior. The protocol: \emph{canonical flux
selection} solves parsimonious FBA --- minimize the $L_1$ total
flux at the fixed biomass optimum --- for the wild type and every
knockout, reads the growth rate from the solution vector at the
biomass reaction, and declares labels at $5\%$ of the level's own
canonical wild type. Labels are unchanged by construction (the
biomass optimum is preserved to $10^{-6}$ at every level; the
plain and canonical arms agree at $\kappa\ge0.991$ table-wide),
and the vertex it selects is unique wherever the parsimony stage
is discriminating. Two solver-honesty provisions are recorded in
the deposited artifacts: a simplex time limit guards the $L_1$
stage (a handful of at-optimum LPs cycle without it at the extreme
maintenance level), with timed-out or numerically failed solves
falling back to the plain optimum vertex --- label-preserving,
flagged per gene --- and the per-level degeneracy signature
(at-optimum flux variability) is logged alongside every sweep.

\begin{proposition}[Multi-axis canonical-selection verdict]
\label{prop:canonical-multiaxis}
Across the six perturbation axes --- nitrogen, oxygen, phosphate,
iron, the non-medium ATP-maintenance axis, and the glucose-only
carbon correction at the baselines --- thirty-one probe levels in
the two reconstructions, with the plain and canonical arms run in
parallel at every level (deposited sweeps and control records):
(i)~plain-FBA transitive calibration collapses at deep limitation
on every supply axis ($r$ from $+0.949$ down to $-0.122$; at the
five deepest supply floors $|r|\le0.11$) and at the maintenance
extreme ($r=+0.453$ iJO1366, $+0.234$ with AUC $0.563$ iML1515);
(ii)~canonical selection restores the transitive association to
$r\ge+0.856$ with held-out ROC AUC $\ge0.978$ at every supply-axis
level in both models (twenty-four levels) and at every maintenance
level in iJO1366
($r\ge+0.937$, AUC $\ge0.982$), the canonical rankings correlated
$+0.62$--$+0.97$ with the baseline canonical ranking;
(iii)~label agreement between the arms is $\kappa\ge0.991$
table-wide ($\kappa=1.000$ at twenty-three of thirty-one levels);
(iv)~the single corner where canonical selection does not restore
the transitive correlation --- the maintenance axis in iML1515 at
$-90\%$ ($r=+0.476$) --- is the measured near-tie boundary of
Remark~\ref{rem:canonical-scope}, and even there the ranking
separation (AUC $0.978$), MCC $0.867$, and labels ($\kappa=0.992$)

\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[The near-tie boundary of canonical selection]
\label{rem:canonical-scope}
The maintenance axis in iML1515 localizes where canonical
selection stops being sufficient. At elevated \texttt{ATPM} the
parsimony stage itself carries \emph{near-tied} optima: vertices
$\approx200$ apart in the squared-flux metric whose $L_1$
objectives differ by $8.6\times10^{-4}$ absolute ($1.2\times10^{-6}$
relative --- within the simplex optimality tolerance), so the
returned vertex is solver-path-dependent. The signature is sharp:
roughly $700$ of $1{,}129$ compensable knockouts record the same
near-tie floor $kV\approx200.01$ (identical to four decimals, ten
changed reactions, gene-intrinsic and level-independent), while
the remaining compensables sit at $kV\approx0$; the supply axes in
the same model show no floor at all (compensable median $kV$
$0.00$--$0.10$). The floor decorrelates the transitive statistic
--- canonical $r$ caps near $+0.48$ at the $-90\%$ endpoint ---
without touching the essentiality ranking (AUC $0.978$, labels
$\kappa=0.992$). Canonical selection is therefore \emph{necessary}
on every axis (plain FBA collapses at deep limitation everywhere,
including the zero-degeneracy maintenance axis), and
\emph{sufficient} precisely where the parsimony stage is
discriminating: all five supply axes in both reconstructions and
the maintenance axis in iJO1366. This also revises the closing
reading rule of Remark~\ref{rem:keio-n-invariance}: wild-type
flux-variability uniqueness (zero \texttt{PGI} width at every
maintenance level) does \emph{not} license plain-FBA readings ---
per-knockout vertex instability at near-zero-biomass optima
defeats it --- so the licensed protocol is canonical selection
throughout, with the near-tie corner reported as its bounded
residual.
\end{remark}
