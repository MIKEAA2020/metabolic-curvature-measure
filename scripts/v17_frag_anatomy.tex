\subsection{Anatomy of one switch}\label{sec:anatomy}

The association of \S\ref{sec:v5} is statistical; this subsection
opens it up and names its parts. The switch is carbon depletion
itself --- the progressive glucose decline of the reference
trajectory --- and its anatomy has three layers, each computable
from the deposited artifacts.

\emph{Which chemistry reroutes.} The curvature mass of the sweeps
concentrates on the forks of central carbon
(\S\ref{sec:walls}), and the reactions carrying the largest mass
are the enzymes of those forks: glucose-6-phosphate isomerase,
phosphofructokinase, fructose-bisphosphate aldolase, and the
pentose-phosphate entry block. The hexose-phosphate pool, the
triose-phosphate pool, the PEP/pyruvate fork, and the anaplerotic
TCA forks are where the rerouting of carbon depletion physically
happens.

\emph{Which regulons the genes belong to.} Partitioning the panel
by $\kmu$ quartile and testing the top quartile ($110$ of the
$424$ nonzero-$\kmu$ genes) for enrichment against the PRECISE
transcriptional-regulatory annotations \citep{sastry2019}, with
the panel as background, gives a precise answer
(Table~\ref{tab:regulons}): the global carbon and energy regulons.
CRP targets are enriched $2.4$-fold, with $56$ of the $89$ CRP
panel targets in the top quartile (Benjamini--Hochberg $q = 8.6
\times 10^{-16}$); Cra, Fur, Fnr, RpoS, Fis, ArcA, NarL, IHF,
RpoD, and NagC follow; eleven regulons survive control at $q <
0.10$. The enrichment is induction-specific. CRP's activation
targets carry high curvature (median $\kmu$ $0.64$ against $0.02$
for the rest of the panel; Mann--Whitney $p = 0.0016$), while its
repression targets do not ($p = 0.43$): induction prices the
option set; repression does not track the geometry.

At operon granularity the pattern is stronger. Whole operons sit
entirely in the top curvature quartile: the NADH dehydrogenase
operon \emph{nuoABCEFGHIJKLMN} ($13$ of its $13$ panel genes; $q =
4.3 \times 10^{-7}$), the ATP synthase operon \emph{atpIBEFHAGDC}
($9$ of $9$), the succinate dehydrogenase operon
\emph{sdhCDAB-sucABCD} ($8$ of $8$), cytochrome \emph{bo}
oxidase \emph{cyoABCDE} ($4$ of $4$), and \emph{manXYZ},
\emph{ptsHI-crr}, and \emph{pdhR-aceEF-lpd} ($3$ of $3$ each).
Within-operon curvature is near-uniform: the mean
within-operon $|\Delta\kmu|$ is $0.044$, against $1.045$ across
operons ($381$ pairs; permutation $p < 2 \times 10^{-4}$). The
control that makes this non-trivial is the GPR structure itself.
Restricted to intra-operon pairs catalyzing \emph{disjoint}
reactions --- genuinely different enzymes, such as the
\emph{sdh} gene products against the \emph{suc} gene products ---
the gap remains $0.082$ against $1.045$ ($205$ pairs,
permutation $p < 2 \times 10^{-4}$), while pairs sharing a
reaction (enzyme-complex subunits; $176$ pairs) are trivially
identical. Co-transcribed units carry near-uniform curvature
across their different enzymes: the option set is priced in
pre-packaged units. The co-expression modules that light up in the
iModulon annotations are the carbon and energy ones (DhaR/Mlc,
Crp-2, ArcA-1, ArcA-2) --- the same sector again.

\emph{What growth sees of it.} Nothing. On all three trajectories
of \S\ref{sec:v7}, the growth-attributed curvature is identically
zero --- $0$ of the $433$ panel genes has nonzero $\kappa_c$ on
any path --- and the value layer carries between $0.024\%$ and
$0.145\%$ of the flux layer's curvature mass. The association of
\S\ref{sec:v5} is therefore carried entirely by growth-invisible
wall mass. Transcriptional induction during carbon depletion
concentrates on the sector where selection on growth is silent:
the zero-cost pathway substitutions of Theorem~\ref{thm:coupling}(i),
which is precisely the degeneracy's design space.

Put together, the anatomy closes a loop that
\citet{kochanowski2013} opened from the other side. They showed
that the response to carbon limitation is coordinated largely at
the metabolite level, through Crp and local metabolite
concentrations; here the regulon that governs the response is the
regulon whose operons carry the curvature, and its enzymes sit on
the fork metabolites that carry the walls. One chemical corollary
follows, as a prediction: of the $237$ regulators in the
transcriptional network, the sensing molecules of five are
themselves metabolites of the reconstruction (FMN, L-tryptophan,
adenosylcobalamin, molybdopterin, spermidine); the
metabolite-effectors of the enriched regulons should prove to be
wall-adjacent pools from Table~\ref{tab:walls}.

\emph{Provenance and honesty.} The enrichment shares the
correlational design and the expression-level confound structure
of the parent association (\S\ref{sec:v5}); the
activation/repression split partially controls for direction of
effect, but expression-matched per-gene controls are future work,
and the PRECISE RNA-seq arms replicate the association only weakly
(\S\ref{sec:v5}), so the magnitude of the enrichment is
platform-dependent even where its sign is consistent.
